chapter one

It all started at Madrasa… 🌸

01 — our beginning

We first met at madrasa when we were little. At that time, we didn't talk much. We were just two people who happened to be in the same place.

Later, I went to another madrasa, and after a few years, somehow life brought us back together. We joined the same madrasa again.

That second beginning was different. Our friendship slowly became stronger than before. We started sharing more things, spending more time together, and becoming an important part of each other's lives.

and I’m so glad it did ♡

chapter four

The day I couldn't stop crying… 💔

One day, because of a problem at madrasa, our teacher asked only some of us to study at her house. She was still at the madrasa. I couldn't handle being separated from her. I cried a lot. She cried too.

Then, somehow, we spoke to our teacher and asked her to let us attend the class together again. And she agreed. I didn't know anything about it. That evening, when I went to class and suddenly saw her there again… I can't explain that happiness. It was a kind of happiness that words cannot describe. For a few days, everything felt perfect again.

♡♥♡♥

distance

Even distance couldn't change us… 🌙

Home
Her Home
♡

One day, my family told me that I should stop going to madrasa. That was very difficult for me. I couldn't handle the thought of not seeing her anymore. That was when she gave me her mobile number. My family allowed me to visit only once a week, so slowly we started talking through calls. Days passed. Then she also stopped coming to class. I didn't know what to do. But somehow, our friendship continued.

chapter six

27 Kilometers for One Person

Home → 27 KM → Her Home ❤️

Once, I travelled around 27 kilometers just to see her. When I reached her house, she was sleeping. Her mother told me that she had a fever. I asked her mother to wake her up, but she didn't. So I just saw her, talked with her mother for a little while, and came back.

The next day, when we spoke on the phone, she told me: “You came to see me… I was really happy.” That one sentence meant so much to me.

my birthday letter to you

My Best One, Thangoo ❤️

You are not just my friend. You are one of the most special people in my life.

We have gone through so many things together — happiness, fights, misunderstandings, distance, tears, and beautiful memories. Sometimes I thought I was losing you. Sometimes other people told me that you had forgotten me. But my heart always knew one thing… You would never forget me. And I never forgot you either.

I don't want our friendship to exist only in this world. I want this friendship to continue in the hereafter too. 🤍

You are my best one, Thangoo. Stay happy just like you are now, always. I will always be there for you. Not only when everything is perfect… but also when life becomes difficult.

I didn't forget you. And I never will. I miss you, Thangoo. ❤️ One day, definitely, we will meet again. Until then, don't feel bad about the distance. I will make dua for you. Take care of yourself. Study well. And whenever you are free… call me. ❤️

Happy Birthday, my best one. 🎂 May Allah bless you with happiness, peace, success, and a beautiful life. And may our friendship remain forever. 🤍

— From the person who will never forget you ❤️
